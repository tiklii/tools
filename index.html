Web Novel Translation Tools

  • EPUB Text Chunker

    Splits large EPUB, TXT, or Markdown chapters into size-limited chunks to easily copy and translate using ChatGPT or other LLMs.

  • TXT to EPUB Converter

    Converts .txt webnovel downloads offered on some chinese websites (sudugu.com) to epub.

  • Web Novel Scraper (Userscript)

    An AI-slop userscript designed to manually scrape chapters. Worked on booktoki.
